## Open

Start with the question the room actually has: who approved this, and how do you know it wasn't
just a button click in the app? Set up the Hero Shield claim as the running example — a claim gets
filed by an agent, not a human clicking "submit."

## The demo

Walk through CIBA email as the approval channel: the agent proposes an action, Auth0 sends the
request to a human out-of-band, and the approval is a real grant — not a stored flag your backend
trusts blindly. Pause on the moment the board member accepts; that's the thing worth narrating
slowly.

## Close

Land on the reusable idea: agent identity needs the same out-of-band, human-verifiable step that
high-value human transactions already require. Point to the Hero Shield and CIBA Email demos for
anyone who wants to run this themselves.
